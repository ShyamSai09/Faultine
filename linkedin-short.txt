# Short version — under 800 characters, per the content guide.

An on-call alert fired with no deploy in 30 days. The agent linked it to two
outages from March and June, flagged two *other* services running the same broken
config, and pointed at a postmortem action written twice and closed never.

Vector search can't do that. It finds the postmortem that resembles your alert —
it can't join that March incident to an unclosed action item in a separate memory
bank, or tell you the answer sentence didn't exist until the memory formed it.

[Hindsight](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/) consolidates facts into
evidence-backed observations, so "which services are still exposed" is a belief
the bank holds and rewrites, not a document you retrieve.

Scored against a hand-labelled key: 83%, miss included. Same model, memory off,
zero prior incidents, no runbook.
